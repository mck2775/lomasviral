Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Who we are

Lomasviral (the "App") is a personal tool operated by Jorge Lopez (the "Operator"). Contact: mck2775@gmail.com.

What data the App accesses

The App is connected only to the Operator's own accounts. Depending on the permissions granted by the Operator, it may access:

  • Facebook Page data of the Operator's own Page: Page identifier and name, Page insights (for example, views and engagement totals), and the list of the Page's Reels with their identifiers and creation dates.
  • YouTube channel data of the Operator's own channel: video identifiers, titles, publication dates, and public statistics (views, likes, comments) plus audience analytics (views, average view duration and percentage, shares, subscribers gained).
  • TikTok account data of the Operator's own account: video list and public statistics.

The App does not access data of other users, does not read private messages, and does not collect contact lists.

How the data is used

The data is used only to measure the performance of the Operator's own content, so that the Operator can compare publications across platforms. It is not used for advertising, for profiling people, or for any purpose unrelated to the App.

Storage and security

Metrics are stored in a private data workspace controlled by the Operator. Access tokens are kept in an encrypted secrets store and are never published, displayed or shared.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share this data with third parties, except where required by law.

Retention and deletion

Metric snapshots are kept while the App is in use. To request deletion of the data, or to revoke the App's access, contact us at the address above. You can also remove the App at any time from your Facebook or Google account settings.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
