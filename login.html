Lomasviral

Lomasviral is a personal tool. The operator signs in with Facebook to measure the performance of their own Facebook Page content (views and engagement) and to compare it with their YouTube and TikTok results.

Step 1. Sign in with Facebook and grant the permissions below.

  • pages_show_list: list the Pages you manage.
  • pages_read_engagement: read your Page's engagement.
  • read_insights: read your Page's insights (views).
  • pages_manage_posts and pages_read_user_content: publish and read your own Reels.
