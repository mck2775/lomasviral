Lomasviral

Lomasviral is a personal tool for the owner of a TikTok account. It helps that owner understand how their own videos perform and publish new videos to the same account. It is not a public service and it does not work with other people's accounts.

What the app does

  • Account overview: reads the basic profile and statistics of the authorized account (display name, follower count, following count, likes and number of videos).
  • Performance review: reads the list of the account's own public videos and their public metrics, so the owner can compare what works.
  • Publishing to the same account: uploads a video to the authorized account through the TikTok Content Posting API. Every upload is started by the account owner.

How it works

  1. The account owner presses Connect TikTok below.
  2. TikTok shows its own consent screen (TikTok Login Kit). The owner chooses whether to grant each permission.
  3. After consent, TikTok returns a single-use authorization code that is exchanged on the server. The client secret is never published on this website.
  4. The app uses the granted permissions only for the functions listed above.

Data the app uses

Only the permissions the owner grants are used: user.info.profile, user.info.stats, video.list, video.upload and video.publish. The app does not collect data about other TikTok users through the TikTok API. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Your control

You can revoke the app's access at any time from the TikTok app settings. You can also ask for deletion of data held by the app by contacting the operator below.
