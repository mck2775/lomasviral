Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Who we are

Lomasviral ("the App") is a personal tool operated by Jorge Lopez ("the Operator"). Contact: mck27752@gmail.com.

What data the App accesses

When the Operator authorizes the App through TikTok Login Kit, the App may access, depending on the scopes granted:

  • basic profile information of the authorizing TikTok account (profile name, bio, verification status);
  • account statistics (follower count, following count, likes count, video count);
  • the list of the account's public videos and their public metrics.

If the Content Posting API is authorized, the App can upload videos to the authorizing account when the account owner starts an upload.

How the data is used

The data is used only to measure the performance of the authorizing account's content and to publish content to that same account. It is not used for advertising, profiling of other people, or any purpose unrelated to the App.

Storage and security

Data is stored in a private cloud data workspace controlled by the Operator. Access tokens and credentials are kept in an encrypted secrets store and are never published.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share TikTok data with third parties, except where required by law.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept while the App is in use. The authorizing user can revoke the App's access at any time from the TikTok app settings, and can request deletion of any data held by the App by writing to the contact address above. Deletion requests are handled within 30 days.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest revision.
